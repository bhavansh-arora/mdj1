Borrowing

How much could you borrow?

Most lenders start at around 4 to 4.5 times income. Some go higher for certain professions or larger incomes, and all of them check your outgoings and credit history.

  • Add a second applicant's income for joint mortgages
  • Buying power adds your deposit to the loan
  • Loan-to-value (LTV) affects the rates you're offered
