About our fees

Fee questions

When do I pay?
We'll confirm when any fee is due at your first meeting, before you commit to anything. Your first consultation is always free.
Why might my fee be different from £295?
The precise amount depends on your circumstances and how complex your case is. It will never exceed £1,000 or 3% of the loan, whichever is lower.
Are there other costs when buying a home?
Usually, yes: solicitor's fees, survey costs, any lender arrangement fee and Stamp Duty Land Tax where it applies. We'll talk through the likely total so you can budget.
What about equity release fees?
Fees for equity release are confirmed at your first appointment.
