How we help

Cover we can arrange

We'll look at what you already have through work before suggesting anything new.

Life insurance

Pays out a lump sum if you die during the policy, so your family can clear or keep paying the mortgage.

Critical illness cover

Pays out if you're diagnosed with a serious illness listed in the policy.

Income protection

Replaces part of your income if illness or injury stops you working. Especially important if you're self-employed.

Home insurance

Buildings and contents cover, which most lenders require from the day you complete.

Free review

When to review your cover

Life changes, and cover that was right five years ago may not be now.

  • You're buying a home or remortgaging
  • You've had a baby or started a family
  • You've become self-employed
  • You've borrowed more on your mortgage
  • Your employer benefits have changed
  • You've never checked what cover you have

Protection FAQs

More answers on our FAQ page, or message Mark on 07798 819741.

Do I need life insurance to get a mortgage?
Usually not, but it's strongly recommended if anyone depends on your income to pay the mortgage.
I have death-in-service at work. Is that enough?
It may help, but it usually ends if you leave that job. We'll look at what you have and whether there's a gap.
How much does cover cost?
It depends on your age, health, smoking status, the amount and the term. We'll get quotes from several insurers so you can compare.
