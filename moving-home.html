Moving home FAQs

More answers on our FAQ page, or message Mark on 07798 819741.

Can I take my mortgage with me?
Most mortgages are portable, which means you can move your current deal to a new property. The lender will still assess you again, and any extra borrowing may be on a different rate.
What if I'm still in a fixed rate?
Leaving early can mean an early repayment charge. Porting usually avoids it. We'll compare both routes, including the charge.
Should I sell before I buy?
Not necessarily, but having a buyer makes your offer stronger. We'll get your mortgage agreed in principle so you're ready to move quickly either way.
